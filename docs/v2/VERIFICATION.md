# V2 verification and operating contract

The bounded SoC qualification bench instantiates the unchanged current production SoC, all23 filelist RTL sources, real CSR/job adapter/serial engine/cache/DMA/packer/writer and the independent accepted logical-row fixture generator. Original current SoC benches, reference rules, LUT and historical source/report hashes remain intact. No datapath completion is mocked and no property assumes complete KV pairs.

The [SV observers](../../sim/v2/soc/tb_soc.sv) retain every original assertion and add actual accepted final-OKAY-B/source retirement before success, complete pairs in both physical groups at top-generated commit, synchronized group validity/length/epoch and every valid result word remaining bit-identical from child publication through ACK. The [host scoreboard](../../sim/v2/soc/tb_soc.cpp) retains independent accepted AR/AW/W/B accounting, exact external bytes, CSR counter/status/IRQ checks, cache/read/store/score monitors and original stress/fault/reset scenarios. It also rejects a new accepted command until the prior owner has reached idle after ACK and checks success IRQ/output publication cannot precede final B in this host sequence.

[Timing control](../../sim/v2/soc/timing.hpp) uses standard MT19937 with explicit modulo selection and recorded seeds. Seed0 preserves canonical timing; nonzero seeds vary independent AW/W readiness, AR readiness, gaps before RVALID, per-burst B delay, CSR AW/W ordering/separation and CSR B/R response stalls. Ready denial is bounded to seven consecutive samples; extra B delay is0..8 cycles. Once RVALID is offered, it is held through acceptance. A configured final-B hold separately leaves compute/result ownership live while successful host DONE/IRQ/output publication must stay absent. The original same-edge completion/IRQ-clear race deliberately retains its exact directed host timing. These finite schedules are not all possible AXI interleavings, a fairness proof or hardware timing measurements.

`prefill/focus/warm/stress/append_fault` cover both fixture populations, causal/noncausal ownership, all four heads/two groups, capacity64, rejected overflow, changed-data CLEAR/repopulation, fatal read/write responses, malformed RLAST and selected reset stages. The accepted command/read-write endpoint are reset together. External writes before a BRESP fault need not roll back. Independent endpoint reset/outstanding drain, physical asynchronous reset closure, gate equivalence, full four-state/URG rerun and unbounded integrated proof remain outside bounded SoC qualification.

Every positive invocation must have one simulator PASS, nonzero successful jobs/exact words and one safety record. Wrong-final-word and missing-fixture negative executions must have nonzero exit, the specific detector and no PASS. `--test-control` exercises the normal runner failure path; it cannot turn a failed verification into PASS. All simulator/compiler timeouts or missing/duplicate PASS records fail the runner. Counts are sampled simulation observations, not new line/FSM/condition/toggle/assertion coverage percentages. The prior24-step cache and12-step abstract host proofs and INCONCLUSIVE deeper attempts retain their original scope/status.
